# Northbridge Bank Fraud Prevention FAQ

Version 1.2 | Effective January 15, 2024

## Will Northbridge Bank ever ask for my PIN, password, or one-time passcode?

No. Northbridge Bank will never ask for your full PIN, online banking password, or a one-time passcode (OTP) by phone, text, or email. If someone asks, hang up and call the number on the back of your card.

## How do I report suspected fraud?

Call the 24/7 fraud line at 1-800-555-0142. You can also lock your card in the mobile app (Cards > Lock Card) and report the transaction in the app under Transactions > Report a Problem.

## I clicked on a suspicious link. What should I do?

1. Change your online banking password immediately from a trusted device.
2. Call the fraud line to have us review your account.
3. Forward the suspicious message to phishing@northbridgebank.example and delete it.
4. Run a security scan on your device.

## What alerts can I set up?

You can enable push, text, or email alerts for: transactions over an amount you choose (default $100), card-not-present purchases, international transactions, password changes, and low balance. Fraud alerts from Northbridge are always sent by text from short code 62758 and never ask you to share a passcode.

## What is account takeover and how do I know if it has happened?

Account takeover occurs when someone gains access to your online banking credentials. Warning signs include password-change emails you did not request, unfamiliar payees, and login alerts from new devices. If you suspect it, call the fraud line right away so we can freeze online access and issue new credentials.

## I sent money to a scammer using NB Send (our person-to-person payments service). Can I get it back?

Payments you authorize yourself, even when you were tricked, are generally not covered by Zero Liability Protection because they are not "unauthorized." Report the payment immediately; we will try to contact the receiving bank, but recovery is not guaranteed. Wire transfers are final once sent.

## How long does a fraud investigation take?

We review unauthorized transaction reports and confirm next steps within 10 business days. Provisional credit timelines for card disputes are described in the Card Dispute Policy.

## How can I protect my accounts?

Use a unique password, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) in Settings > Security, keep your contact information current, review your statements monthly, and never share verification codes.

## Does Northbridge Bank call customers about fraud?

Yes, but our fraud team will only ask you to confirm recent transactions. We will never ask you to move money, read back a passcode, or install software. If you are unsure, hang up and call us at 1-800-555-0142.
