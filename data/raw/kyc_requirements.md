# Northbridge Bank Identity Verification (KYC) Requirements

Version 2.0 | Effective January 1, 2024

Federal law (the USA PATRIOT Act and the Bank Secrecy Act) requires us to verify the identity of every person who opens an account. This is called Know Your Customer (KYC).

## 1. Individuals: Required Information and Documents

When opening an account we collect your full legal name, date of birth, residential address, and Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN).

You must also provide:

1. **One valid government-issued photo ID**: a driver's license, state ID, passport, or military ID. The ID must not be expired.
2. **Proof of address** if the address on your ID does not match your current address. Acceptable proof includes a utility bill, lease agreement, or bank statement dated within the last **90 days**.

## 2. Non-U.S. Citizens

Non-U.S. citizens must provide a valid passport and one of the following: a visa, a permanent resident card, an employment authorization document, or a foreign national ID. A U.S. address is required to open an account.

## 3. Minors

Customers under 18 must open an account jointly with a parent or legal guardian, who must complete full KYC. The minor's SSN and a birth certificate or school ID are required.

## 4. Business Accounts

Businesses must provide:

- Employer Identification Number (EIN)
- Formation documents (articles of incorporation or organization, or a partnership agreement)
- Business license where applicable
- Identification of every person who owns **25% or more** of the business (beneficial owners), and one person with significant control (such as a CEO or managing member)

## 5. Verification Timeline

Most identities are verified instantly. If we need manual review, verification can take up to **3 business days**. Until verification is complete, the account may be restricted to deposits only.

## 6. Periodic Re-verification

We refresh customer information periodically:

- Standard-risk customers: every **3 years**.
- High-risk customers (including politically exposed persons and certain business types): every **12 months**.

You may be asked to supply updated ID or income information. Failure to respond within 30 days may result in account restrictions.

## 7. Enhanced Due Diligence and Reporting

- Cash transactions over **$10,000** in a single business day are reported to the government on a Currency Transaction Report (CTR). This is routine and does not imply wrongdoing.
- Deliberately splitting cash transactions to avoid the $10,000 threshold ("structuring") is illegal and will be reported as suspicious activity.
- Politically exposed persons (PEPs) and their close associates are subject to enhanced due diligence, including source-of-funds review.
