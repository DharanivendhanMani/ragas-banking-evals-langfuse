# Northbridge Bank Merchant Chargeback Guide

Version 1.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

## Quick Summary

- A chargeback is a reversal of a card payment initiated through the card network after you dispute a purchase.
- Merchants have **10 business days** to respond to a chargeback after we send it.
- You should expect a final decision within 45 to 90 days of filing.
- You can submit supporting evidence at any time while the case is open.

## When a Chargeback Applies

Chargebacks are available for: goods or services not received, items significantly different from the description, duplicate charges, charges after cancellation, and charges for incorrect amounts. They are generally not available for buyer's remorse or purchases that match the merchant's stated return policy.

## Detailed Process

1. **You file a dispute.** Provide the date, amount, merchant name, and what went wrong. Attach receipts, order confirmations, and messages with the merchant.
2. **Northbridge reviews** the claim and, where appropriate, sends the chargeback to the merchant's bank through the card network.
3. **Merchant response window.** The merchant has **14 calendar days** from the date the chargeback is received to accept the chargeback or present evidence that the transaction was valid (this is called "representment").
4. **If the merchant accepts**, the amount is permanently credited to your account.
5. **If the merchant represents**, we review the evidence and contact you with it. You then have 10 calendar days to respond.
6. **Arbitration.** If the dispute is still unresolved, either side may escalate to arbitration through the card network, which can take up to 45 additional days. The network's decision is final.

## Tips

- Keep all receipts and correspondence.
- Cancel recurring subscriptions in writing before disputing, to strengthen your case.
- Do not issue a chargeback and also request a refund from the merchant; a double refund may be reversed.
