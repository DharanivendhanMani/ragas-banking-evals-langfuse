# Northbridge Bank Savings and Certificate of Deposit (CD) Rates

Rates effective July 1, 2024 | Version 2024-07

## 1. Savings Accounts (variable rates)

| Account | Balance tier | APY |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Savings | All balances | 0.40% |
| High-Yield Savings | Less than $1,000 | 0.50% |
| High-Yield Savings | $1,000 and above | 4.15% |

Savings rates are variable and may change at any time without notice. Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## 2. Certificates of Deposit (fixed rates)

| Term | APY | Minimum deposit |
|---|---|---|
| 3 months | 3.80% | $1,000 |
| 6 months | 4.25% | $1,000 |
| 12 months | 4.50% | $1,000 |
| 24 months | 4.20% | $1,000 |
| 60 months | 3.90% | $1,000 |

CD rates are fixed for the entire term. The maximum deposit per CD is $500,000.

## 3. Early Withdrawal Penalties

If you withdraw from a CD before maturity, a penalty applies:

- CD terms of **12 months or less**: 90 days of interest.
- CD terms **longer than 12 months**: 180 days of interest.

If you have not earned enough interest to cover the penalty, the difference is deducted from your principal.

## 4. Renewal and Grace Period

At maturity, your CD renews automatically for the same term at the rate in effect on the renewal date unless you instruct us otherwise. You have a **10 calendar day grace period** after maturity to withdraw funds or change the term without penalty.

## 5. Interest Payment Options

CD interest can be paid monthly (to a linked Northbridge account) or at maturity. Interest on CDs with terms of 12 months or less is paid at maturity only.

## 6. Insurance

Deposits at Northbridge Bank are insured by the FDIC up to $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category.
