# Northbridge Bank Deposit Account Terms and Conditions

Version 3.0 | Effective January 1, 2024

## 1. Account Types

Northbridge Bank offers the following personal deposit accounts:

- **Everyday Checking**: a standard checking account for daily banking.
- **Premier Checking**: a checking account for customers with higher balances, with enhanced limits and benefits.
- **Student Checking**: for customers aged 16 to 24 with proof of enrollment. Customers under 18 must open the account jointly with a parent or legal guardian.
- **Basic Savings**: a savings account with a variable interest rate.
- **High-Yield Savings**: a tiered-rate savings account. See the Savings and CD Rates document for current rates.

Monthly maintenance fees and the ways to waive them are listed in the Fee Schedule.

## 2. Opening an Account

To open a personal account you must be at least 18 years old (except Student Checking, see above) and complete identity verification as described in the KYC Requirements document.

Minimum opening deposits:

- Everyday Checking: $25
- Premier Checking: $100
- Student Checking: $0
- Basic Savings: $50
- High-Yield Savings: $500

## 3. Deposits and Funds Availability

- **Direct deposits** are available on the business day we receive them.
- **Cash deposited** at a branch teller is available immediately.
- **Checks**: the first $225 of a check deposit is available on the next business day. The remainder is generally available on the second business day after the deposit.
- **Mobile check deposit** limits are $5,000 per day and $10,000 per rolling 30 days. Checks deposited through the mobile app before 8:00 PM Eastern Time are generally available on the next business day.
- A hold of up to 7 business days may be placed on new accounts (open less than 30 days), large deposits over $5,525 in a single day, or checks that appear questionable.

## 4. Savings Withdrawal Limits

Savings accounts are limited to six outgoing transfers or withdrawals (online, phone, or automatic) per monthly statement cycle. Additional requests may be declined. Withdrawals made in person at a branch or ATM are not counted toward this limit.

## 5. Inactive and Dormant Accounts

- An account with no customer-initiated activity for 12 months is classified as **inactive**.
- After 24 months of no activity, the account is classified as **dormant**, and we will attempt to contact you.
- If an account remains dormant for 36 months total and we cannot reach you, the balance may be transferred to the state as unclaimed property (escheatment), as required by law.

## 6. Closing an Account

You may close an account at any time by visiting a branch or calling customer service. If you close an account within 90 days of opening, an early closure fee applies (see the Fee Schedule). Any remaining balance is returned by check or transfer within 10 business days. Northbridge Bank may close an account with 30 days' written notice, or immediately in cases of suspected fraud or misuse.

## 7. Joint Accounts

Each joint owner has full authority to deposit, withdraw, and close the account. Each joint owner is jointly and severally liable for any overdrafts or fees.
