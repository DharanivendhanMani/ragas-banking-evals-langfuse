# Northbridge Bank Loan Eligibility Guide

Version 1.4 | Effective February 1, 2024

## 1. General Requirements

To apply for any Northbridge loan you must be at least 18, a U.S. citizen or permanent resident (or hold a valid visa with at least 12 months remaining), have a valid Social Security Number, and have a verifiable source of income. We pull a credit report as part of every application.

## 2. Personal Loans

| Feature | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Loan amount | $2,000 to $50,000 |
| Terms | 12 to 72 months |
| APR range | 8.99% to 19.99% (fixed) |
| Minimum credit score | 660 |
| Maximum debt-to-income (DTI) ratio | 43% |
| Origination fee | 2% of the loan amount, deducted from proceeds |
| Prepayment penalty | None |

Decisions are usually made within 1 to 2 business days. Funds are deposited into your Northbridge account within 1 business day after you sign the loan agreement.

## 3. Auto Loans

- Minimum credit score: **620**.
- Maximum term: **84 months** for new vehicles, **72 months** for used vehicles. Used vehicles must be no more than 10 years old with fewer than 120,000 miles.
- Down payment: at least **10%** of the vehicle price. A larger down payment may improve your rate.
- Maximum DTI: 45%.
- There is no prepayment penalty.

## 4. Mortgages (Conventional)

- Minimum credit score: **680**.
- Minimum down payment: **5%** for primary residences. Investment properties require 20%.
- Maximum DTI: **45%**.
- If your down payment is less than 20%, private mortgage insurance (PMI) is required until your loan-to-value ratio reaches 80%.
- Required documents: two most recent pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns for the last two years, two months of bank statements, and a signed purchase agreement.
- Pre-approval is valid for 90 days.

## 5. How DTI Is Calculated

DTI is your total monthly debt payments (including the proposed new loan) divided by your gross monthly income. For example, a borrower with $6,000 gross monthly income and $2,100 in total monthly debt payments has a DTI of 35%.

## 6. Reasons for Denial

Common reasons for denial include a credit score below the minimum, DTI above the maximum, insufficient or unverifiable income, recent bankruptcy (within 4 years), or an incomplete application. If denied, you will receive a written notice explaining the main reasons and your right to a free copy of your credit report.
