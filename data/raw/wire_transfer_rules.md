# Northbridge Bank Wire Transfer Rules

Version 1.6 | Effective March 1, 2024

## 1. Types of Wires

- **Domestic wires** are sent within the United States and are typically completed the same business day if submitted before the cutoff.
- **International wires** are sent to banks outside the United States and typically take 1 to 5 business days, depending on the destination country and currency.

## 2. Cutoff Times (Eastern Time, business days only)

| Wire type | Cutoff |
|---|---|
| Domestic outgoing | 4:00 PM ET |
| International outgoing | 2:00 PM ET |
| Incoming wire credit | 5:00 PM ET |

Wires submitted after the cutoff, or on weekends and federal holidays, are processed the next business day.

## 3. Fees

- Domestic outgoing wire: **$25**
- International outgoing wire: **$45** (some intermediary or receiving banks may deduct additional charges)
- Incoming domestic wire: **$15**
- Incoming international wire: **$20**

## 4. Daily Limits

| Channel | Everyday Checking | Premier Checking |
|---|---|---|
| Online / mobile banking | $25,000 per day | $100,000 per day |
| Branch | $100,000 per day | $250,000 per day |

Higher limits may be available on request with additional verification. Limits may be reduced on newly opened accounts (under 30 days).

## 5. Information Required to Send a Wire

For all wires: the beneficiary's full name and address, the beneficiary's account number, and the receiving bank's name.

- **Domestic**: the receiving bank's 9-digit ABA routing number.
- **International**: the receiving bank's SWIFT/BIC code, and the IBAN where required by the destination country.

## 6. Security and Verification

- Wires over **$10,000** requested online require a callback verification to the phone number on file or approval with a one-time passcode.
- We will never ask you to send a wire to "secure" your account. Do not send a wire to anyone who contacted you unexpectedly.

## 7. Cancelling or Recalling a Wire

A wire is **final and irrevocable once sent**. You may submit a recall request by calling 1-800-555-0177. Recalls are on a best-effort basis, work best when requested within 24 hours, and depend on the receiving bank agreeing to return the funds. The wire fee is not refunded.
