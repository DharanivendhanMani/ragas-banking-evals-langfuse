# Northbridge Bank Card Dispute and Unauthorized Transaction Policy

Version 2.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

## 1. Scope

This policy covers disputes on Northbridge debit cards and credit cards, including unauthorized transactions, billing errors, and merchant disputes.

## 2. How to Report

Report a lost, stolen, or compromised card immediately:

- Lock the card instantly in the mobile app (Cards > Lock Card).
- Call the 24/7 card services line at 1-800-555-0142.
- Visit any branch.

To dispute a transaction, call card services, use the app (Transactions > Report a Problem), or write to Northbridge Bank Disputes, PO Box 4100, Columbus, OH 43216.

## 3. Time Limits for Reporting

- You must report an unauthorized or incorrect transaction within **60 days** of the statement date on which the transaction first appeared.
- Disputes reported after 60 days may still be reviewed, but protections are not guaranteed.

## 4. Customer Liability

Northbridge Zero Liability Protection applies to unauthorized card transactions reported within the 60-day window: you are not responsible for them.

For unauthorized debit card transactions made using a PIN, federal Regulation E rules apply:

- Reported within 2 business days of learning of the loss: maximum liability **$50**.
- Reported after 2 business days but within 60 days of the statement: maximum liability **$500**.
- Reported later than 60 days after the statement: you may be liable for the full amount of transactions occurring after the 60-day period.

## 5. Provisional Credit and Investigation Timeline

**Debit cards**

- We acknowledge your dispute immediately and begin an investigation.
- If the investigation is not complete within **5 business days**, we issue a provisional credit for the disputed amount so that you can use the funds.
- We complete the investigation within **45 calendar days**. This extends to **90 calendar days** for new accounts (open less than 30 days), point-of-sale transactions, and transactions initiated outside the United States.
- If we find no error, we notify you in writing and reverse the provisional credit after giving 5 business days' notice.

**Credit cards**

- We acknowledge a written billing error notice within **30 days**.
- We resolve the dispute within **two billing cycles**, and never later than **90 days**.
- You do not have to pay the disputed amount or related finance charges while the dispute is open.

## 6. Merchant Disputes

For goods not received, services not rendered, or defective goods, first try to resolve the issue with the merchant. If that fails, file a dispute and include receipts, correspondence, and the dates you contacted the merchant. See the Chargeback Guide for how merchant chargebacks work.

## 7. Replacement Cards

After a card is reported lost or stolen, a replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days. Expedited delivery (1 to 2 business days) is available for a fee listed in the Fee Schedule.
