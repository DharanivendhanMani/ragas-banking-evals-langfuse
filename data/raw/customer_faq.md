# Northbridge Bank Customer FAQ

Compiled from support tickets | Last updated: various

## How do I reset my online banking password?

Go to the login page and select "Forgot password". Enter your username and we will send a verification code to your phone on file. Enter the code and choose a new password with at least 12 characters.

## How can I reset my password for online banking?

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot your password?" and follow the steps. A one-time code is sent to your registered phone or email. Your new password must be at least 10 characters long and include a number.

## I forgot my online banking password. What do I do?

Select "Forgot password" on the login page. You'll receive a code by text message. If you no longer have access to your phone number, visit a branch with photo ID.

## How long does a mobile check deposit take to become available?

Checks deposited through the mobile app before 8:00 PM ET are generally available on the next business day.

## When will my mobile deposit be available?

Mobile deposits are typically available within 1 to 2 business days. Deposits made after 8:00 PM ET or on weekends are treated as received on the next business day.

## When will funds from my mobile check deposit show in my account?

Funds from mobile deposits are usually available by the second business day. Large deposits or new accounts may be held longer.

## How do I report a lost or stolen debit card?

Lock your card in the mobile app, then call card services at 1-800-555-0142, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## What number do I call if my card is lost?

Call 1-800-555-0142 any time of day. You can also report it in the mobile app or at a branch.

## What should I do if my debit card is stolen?

Call card services at 1-800-555-0149 as soon as possible and we will block the card and send a replacement within 5 to 7 business days.

## How do I update my mailing address?

Sign in to online banking, go to Profile > Contact Information, and update your address. A confirmation letter is mailed to both the old and new addresses.

## How can I change my address on file?

You can change your address in the mobile app under Settings > Personal Info. For security reasons, a change of address may require additional ID verification.

## Can I get a paper statement?

Yes. Statements are electronic by default. You can request paper statements in Settings > Statements. A monthly paper statement fee applies.

## How do I stop receiving paper statements?

Go to Settings > Statements and choose "Paperless". The paper statement fee will no longer apply from the next statement cycle.
