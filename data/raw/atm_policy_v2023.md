# Northbridge Bank ATM and Debit Card Usage Policy

Version 1.0 | Effective January 1, 2023

## 1. Daily Limits

| Limit | Everyday Checking | Premier Checking | Student Checking |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATM cash withdrawal | $500 per day | $1,000 per day | $300 per day |
| Debit card purchases | $3,000 per day | $5,000 per day | $1,500 per day |
| ATM cash deposit | $2,500 per day | $5,000 per day | $1,000 per day |

## 2. ATM Fees

- Northbridge Bank ATMs and ATMs in the Allpoint network: no fee.
- Other (out-of-network) ATMs in the United States: **$2.50** per transaction, plus any fee charged by the ATM owner.
- ATMs outside the United States: $5.00 per transaction.
- Balance inquiry at an out-of-network ATM: $1.00.

## 3. Card Replacement

Standard replacement of a damaged card is free. Expedited replacement (delivery within 2 business days) costs $20.

## 4. Requesting Higher Limits

You may request a temporary increase to your ATM withdrawal limit at any branch. Increases are valid for up to 7 days and are subject to approval.

## 5. Lost PIN

If you forget your PIN, you can reset it in the mobile app or at a branch with photo ID.
