# Northbridge Bank Overdraft and Non-Sufficient Funds Policy

Version 2.2 | Effective March 1, 2024

## 1. What Is an Overdraft?

An overdraft occurs when you do not have enough available money in your checking account to cover a transaction, and we pay it anyway.

## 2. Standard Overdraft Coverage

- **Checks, ACH payments, and recurring bills** are covered automatically at our discretion.
- **ATM withdrawals and everyday debit card purchases** are only covered if you have opted in to Overdraft Coverage for Debit Cards. Without your opt-in, these transactions are declined at no charge. You can opt in or out at any time in the app (Settings > Overdraft) or at a branch.

## 3. Fees

- **Overdraft fee**: **$34** per item.
- **Maximum of 3 overdraft fees per business day** ($102 total).
- **Non-sufficient funds (NSF) fee**: $34 when we decline and return a check or ACH payment. NSF fees count towards the daily maximum of three combined overdraft and NSF fees.
- **Overdraft Protection transfer fee**: **$10** each time we transfer funds from a linked savings account to cover an overdraft.

## 4. Situations Where No Fee Is Charged

- If your account is overdrawn by **$10 or less** at the end of the business day.
- If you bring your account to a positive balance by the **end of the next business day** (11:59 PM Eastern Time), we automatically refund the overdraft fees from that day. This is called Next-Day Grace.

## 5. Overdraft Protection Options

You can link a savings account, or a Northbridge credit card, to your checking account. Linked savings accounts are used first to cover overdrafts, for a $10 transfer fee per transfer (see Section 3).

## 6. Continuing Negative Balances

If your account stays overdrawn for **30 consecutive days**, Northbridge Bank may close the account and report it to a consumer reporting agency. You remain responsible for repaying the balance.

## 7. How Transactions Are Posted

We post deposits first, then withdrawals in the order they are received (not largest to smallest). Pending transactions reduce your available balance immediately.
