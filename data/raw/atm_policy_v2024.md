# Northbridge Bank ATM and Debit Card Usage Policy

Version 2.0 | Effective March 1, 2024
Supersedes Version 1.0 (January 1, 2023)

## 1. Daily Limits

| Limit | Everyday Checking | Premier Checking | Student Checking |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATM cash withdrawal | $800 per day | $1,500 per day | $400 per day |
| Debit card purchases | $4,000 per day | $7,500 per day | $2,000 per day |
| ATM cash deposit | $3,000 per day | $7,500 per day | $1,000 per day |

## 2. ATM Fees

- Northbridge Bank ATMs and ATMs in the Allpoint network: no fee.
- Other (out-of-network) ATMs in the United States: **$3.00** per transaction, plus any fee charged by the ATM owner.
- ATMs outside the United States: **$5.00** per transaction, plus a 1% foreign currency conversion charge.
- Balance inquiry at an out-of-network ATM: $1.00.

## 3. Card Replacement

Standard replacement of a damaged card is free. Expedited replacement (delivery within 2 business days) costs $25.

## 4. Requesting Higher Limits

You may request a temporary increase to your ATM withdrawal limit through the mobile app or at any branch. Increases are valid for up to 14 days and are subject to approval. Increases above $2,500 require branch verification with photo ID.

## 5. Lost PIN

If you forget your PIN, you can reset it in the mobile app or at a branch with photo ID.

## 6. Card Security Controls

New in this version: you can set custom per-transaction limits and disable international or online use of your debit card in the app under Cards > Controls.
